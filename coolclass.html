Unnecessary Electricity Use

The Real Impact of Classroom Heat

When heat builds up unchecked, schools overcompensate with excessive cooling equipment, initiating an expensive cycle of equipment strain and wasted power.

☀️

Intense Heat Accumulation

Direct sunlight beating through unshaded windows, uninsulated flat concrete roofs, and body heat from 30+ students trap heat inside, creating high thermal loads.

🧠

Learning & Focus Suffer

When temperatures cross 28°C, cognitive performance drops up to 15%. Students and teachers feel tired, sweaty, and lose focus when classrooms are overheated.

💸

The 16°C Panic Setting

Setting the AC to 16°C when a room hits 36°C does not cool the space any faster. Instead, compressors run continuously at maximum load, draining power and spiking costs.

Website-Based Solutions

Classroom Heat & Electricity Waste, Solved Together

Cool Class is a simple, student-led website that helps keep classrooms comfortable without wasting electricity. It shows real-time room temperatures and energy use, shares easy natural cooling tips, sends friendly reminders to switch off lights and fans, and lets classes track their savings together.

Real-Time Energy & Temperature Dashboard

What it does

  • Shows each classroom's current temperature and electricity use in simple graphs
  • Highlights rooms that are too hot or using too much electricity
  • Shows which classrooms have lights/fans running but are empty
How it helps: Everyone sees the problem clearly — teachers, students, and administrators. Awareness alone reduces waste by 15–20%.

Smart Schedule & Reminder System

What it does

  • Matches the class schedule to when fans/lights should be ON or OFF
  • Sends simple reminders before classes end: “Please turn off lights and fans before leaving!”
  • Reminds users: “Open windows instead — fresh air cools the room naturally”
How it helps: Stops electricity from running in empty rooms and encourages natural cooling instead of relying only on electric fans.

Educational Guide & Best Practices Hub

What it does

  • Posts easy tips like:
    • Open windows between classes to flush out hot air
    • Close blinds/curtains when sun hits directly
    • Turn off fans/lights when no one is in the room
    • Use natural light instead of switching on lights
  • Tracks classroom challenges — which ones stay hottest and why
  • Lets classes report problems (e.g., “Fan not working” or “Windows stuck closed”)
How it helps: Teaches the whole school simple habits that lower heat without using more electricity.
